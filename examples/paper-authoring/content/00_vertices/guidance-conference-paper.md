# Conference Paper Guidance

Quality criteria for reviewing and authoring conference papers, organized
by section.

## Abstract

| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Excellent | Self-contained; core contribution in first two sentences; quantifies improvement |
| Good | Self-contained; contribution stated but not quantified |
| Needs work | Requires reading the paper to understand what was done |

## Introduction

| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Excellent | Gap clearly motivated with evidence; claims are falsifiable; scope is bounded |
| Good | Gap stated; claims present but vague |
| Needs work | No clear gap; claims are aspirational rather than falsifiable |

## Methods

| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Excellent | A qualified reader could reproduce the work without contacting the authors |
| Good | Key steps described; some details missing |
| Needs work | High-level description only; not reproducible |

## Results

| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Excellent | Every claim from the Introduction is directly addressed; baselines included |
| Good | Main claims addressed; some claims underdeveloped |
| Needs work | Results do not map back to the stated claims |

## Conclusion

| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Excellent | Summarizes contribution, honestly states limitations, identifies future work |
| Good | Summarizes contribution; limitations mentioned briefly |
| Needs work | Repeats abstract; no limitations discussed |
